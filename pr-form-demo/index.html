Capital Expenditure Request CAPEX FORM REQUIRED

CapEx covers assets with a useful life over 12 months — equipment, machinery, installations, IT hardware, warehouse fixtures, and similar. These hit the balance sheet, not the P&L, and require Finance sign-off in addition to the standard approval chain.

1
Download the CapEx template (disabled in this demo) and open it in Excel.
2
Fill in the CAPEX Request Fields tab — project title, classification, description, purpose, and asset category.
3
Fill in the Line Detail tab with item descriptions, quantities, and costs.
4
Complete and submit this PR form — it flags this as a CapEx request and emails the account buyer.
5
Reply to that email with the completed CapEx Excel attached.
